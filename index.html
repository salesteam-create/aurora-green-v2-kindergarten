What AGG does

Three products, one compliance loop

From vessel onboarding to bankable passport — every step tracked, priced, and ready for class-society review.

Digital Twin Creation

Every vessel becomes a live, component-level replica. Hull, engine, fuel, ballast, bridge, deck, cargo and accommodation are mapped individually so you can see compliance status per zone.

  • Interactive SVG ship explorer
  • Per-component scoring and ownership
  • Asset value tied to compliance state

Certification Engine

AI pre-validates every submission against EU, IMO and class-society rules before it reaches a human reviewer. Gap analysis, cost estimate and timeline ship with every regulation.

  • AI-driven document cross-referencing
  • Cost & timeline estimates per gap
  • Streamlined DNV review queue

Digital Twin Passport

An exportable, signed record of every certification, audit and asset-value milestone. Designed to move cleanly between insurers, lenders, class societies and recycling yards.

  • Exportable PDF & structured data
  • Counter-signed by reviewing entity
  • Shareable with lenders & insurers
How it works

From vessel to passport in four steps

01

Register Your Vessel

Onboard any ship with its IMO number, flag, type and target market. We spin up the digital twin instantly.

02

Build the Digital Twin

Each component — hull, engine, fuel, ballast, bridge — is mapped and assigned to the accountable compliance entity.

03

Run Certification Analysis

AI pre-validates documents against every applicable regulation. DNV reviewers approve from a clean queue.

04

Export & Share Passport

Generate the signed Digital Twin Passport — share with lenders, insurers, class societies or buyers.
